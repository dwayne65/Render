How the system works

Simple steps to recover your belongings or return someone else’s item.

1. Report

Submit a lost or found item with clear details and a photo for faster recognition.

2. Search

Browse listings and filter by category, location, and date to find a match.

3. Claim

Submit a verified claim and track the review process until it is approved or rejected.

Why this platform matters

Trusted handling

Every claim goes through a review process to reduce errors and protect ownership information.

Fast support

Lost items, found items, and claim updates are tracked centrally for the whole campus.

Analytics

Administrators can monitor trends, returned items, and active claims in real time.
